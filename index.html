What you're never charged for

  • Service conversations. A customer writes in, you reply — free on Growth, Pro and Business alike.
  • Click-to-WhatsApp ad conversations. Chats opened from a CTWA ad are free.
  • Utility replies inside the 24-hour service window. Set by the slider above.

How much runs on autopilot?

An automation trigger is counted every time an automation you've set up fires for a customer — a chatbot, keyword action, default action, reply material, routing rule, sequence or WhatsApp Flow. What's assumed here, so you can check it.
  1. Per-message rates come from the Wati Growth, Pro and Business rate cards effective 1 July 2026, and are charged on the recipient's country. Each plan has its own rates — the higher tiers are cheaper per message.
  2. Plan prices in ₹: Growth 2,199 annual / 2,699 monthly, Pro 4,899 / 6,499, Business 14,799 / 18,499. Extra users cost ₹1,299 on Pro and ₹2,199 on Business. Growth includes 3 users and takes no extras.
  3. Growth's 15,000-broadcast monthly cap is checked against marketing plus utility volume. Authentication messages are API-triggered one-to-one and aren't treated as broadcasts. Pro and Business have no broadcast cap.
  4. Included automation triggers: 1,000 on Growth, 2,000 on Pro, 5,000 on Business. Top-ups are ₹1,000 per 1,000 triggers and the estimate rounds up to whole blocks. Unused triggers don't roll over.
  5. Included AI Co-pilot credits: 250 on Growth, 500 on Pro, 1,500 on Business. Top-ups are ₹1,600 per 1,000 credits.
  6. Extra WhatsApp numbers are ₹2,499 each a month and need the Business plan. Shopify is billed in USD at $4.99 a month; the ₹440 shown is an approximate conversion that moves with the exchange rate.
  7. Message charges are drawn from a prepaid wallet, separate from the subscription invoice. Auto-recharge isn't available for rupee accounts, so the wallet needs topping up by hand.
  8. Business volume discounts above roughly a million messages a month aren't modelled. Also excluded: Astra AI agents, dedicated onboarding, technical account management, professional services, green-tick verification and the Blitz high-throughput add-on — all quoted separately.
