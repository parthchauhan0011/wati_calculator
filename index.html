How many messages will you send a month?

WhatsApp charges per delivered message by category, not per contact. Split your monthly outflow across the three categories that cost money. Reaching 15,000 people with a promo and 10,000 with order updates is 15,000 marketing and 10,000 utility.

Question 4

How much of it runs on autopilot?

An automation trigger is counted every time an automation you've set up fires for a customer — a chatbot, keyword action, default action, reply material, routing rule, sequence or WhatsApp Flow. Run out mid-month and automations stop until you buy more, so your team answers by hand. What's assumed here, so you can check it.
  1. Per-message rates come from the Wati Growth and Pro rate cards effective 1 July 2026. Marketing is the only category that differs between plans; utility and authentication are identical.
  2. Business plan rates are a stand-in. Wati publishes a separate Business rate card that wasn't supplied here, and Business also carries volume discounts above roughly a million messages a month. Business currently reuses Pro rates — load the Business card, or edit the Business column, before quoting that tier.
  3. Plan prices are the listed Indian figures: Growth ₹2,199 annual / ₹2,699 monthly, Pro ₹4,899 / ₹6,499, Business ₹14,799 / ₹18,499. Extra users cost ₹1,299 on Pro and ₹2,199 on Business. Growth includes 3 users and takes no extras.
  4. Growth's 15,000-broadcast monthly cap is checked against marketing plus utility volume. Authentication messages are API-triggered one-to-one and aren't treated as broadcasts. Pro and Business have no broadcast cap.
  5. Included automation triggers: 1,000 on Growth, 2,000 on Pro, 5,000 on Business. Top-ups are ₹1,000 per 1,000 triggers and the estimate rounds up to whole blocks. Unused triggers don't roll over.
  6. Included AI Co-pilot credits: 250 on Growth, 500 on Pro, 1,500 on Business. Top-ups are ₹1,600 per 1,000 credits.
  7. Shopify is billed in USD at $4.99 a month; the ₹440 shown is an approximate conversion and moves with the exchange rate.
  8. Message charges are drawn from a prepaid wallet, separate from the subscription invoice. Auto-recharge isn't available for rupee accounts, so the wallet needs topping up by hand.
  9. Not included: Astra AI agents, which are priced separately; dedicated onboarding, technical account management and professional services, which are quoted case by case; green-tick verification; and the Blitz high-throughput add-on.
